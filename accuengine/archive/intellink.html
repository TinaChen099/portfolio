Intellink is an AI-powered procurement platform that helps connect global buyers to suppliers

The company already had a functioning website and was actively running Google Search Ads. Initial campaign performance revealed an interesting insight:

6.5% Click Through Rate Before Redesign
0.5% Conversion Rate Before Redesign

This means that people were interested enough to click the ad, which meant the acquisition strategy and search intent alignment were working. The problem existed after users landed on the website.

The landing page was failing to communicate value clearly enough to convert visitors into active users.

Goal

Redesign the landing page to improve Upper and Mid-Funnel conversion

Let's break apart the current website!

Overall Issue

The page contained lots of information, but not an engaging narrative.

Users were exposed to sections about maps, industries, statistics, and company information without understanding how those pieces connected. As a result, visitors never reached a clear answer to three critical questions:

  • What does Intellink do?
  • Why is it different?
  • What should I do next?

My Strategy

Instead of presenting a list of information, I focus on the buyer’s psychology. What is the visitor thinking about as they go through this journey? What do they anticipate to see next? I design this page to be a complete narrative
