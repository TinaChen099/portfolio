About Me

I didn’t come to AI just because I was fascinated by the technology—I came because I was curious about the people who have to live with it. My work lives at the intersection of innovation and empathy. I ask: What does it take for someone to trust a machine? How do we make complexity feel intuitive? How can we help users feel in control, even when the system is smarter than them? Whether it's healthcare, automotive, or creative tools, I bring a human-centered approach to AI, continuing to serve the users in a future heavily directed by technology.
