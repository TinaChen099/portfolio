THE RIGHT CUSTOMER

Instead of building for both buyers and suppliers, I helped redefine the product around a buyer-first experience based on user research, founder expertise, and monetization potential.

THE MVP

The MVP at this stage is to build a clear path for buyers to search for suppliers in the global market. I started by quickly prototyping a happy path and bringing it to user tests to gain initial reactions.

THE PRODUCT STRATEGY

Transforming a Search Tool into an AI-Native Product

Position Intellink not as another supply chain search tool, but an AI sourcing agent that could deliver meaningful business value at scale.

Product: AI Sourcing Agent

Research showed that buyers often struggled to articulate exactly what they needed, making traditional search an ineffective solution. To address this, I helped transform the product into an AI sourcing agent capable of interpreting user intent, identifying missing requirements, and guiding users toward suitable suppliers.

Series B - Now

How do we evolve into an AI platform?

Scaling an AI Product into an AI Platform

A customizable AI platform to expand the company's long-term market opportunity, maximize valuation, and strengthen international partnerships.

Product: AI Sourcing Platform - Purclaw

Purclaw is an AI sourcing platform that helps buyers manage supplier relationships, automate recurring procurement tasks, analyze and store contracts, and build custom skills that train sourcing agents around their organization’s unique workflows.

Create & Share AI Skills

Build reusable AI skills that automate everyday procurement tasks, from supplier qualification to document analysis. Share them across your organization or publish them to the Skills Marketplace.

AI Workflow Automation

Describe a task in plain language, and Purclaw turns it into a reusable automation. Automatically review RFQs, validate documents, monitor supplier updates, and manage every workflow from a centralized automation hub.

A Unified Supplier Workspace

Manage suppliers, conversations, contracts, and sourcing in one workspace. AI assists throughout the procurement lifecycle, not just during supplier discovery.
